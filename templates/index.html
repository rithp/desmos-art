🎬 Video to Desmos

Converts every sampled frame into one Desmos expression and builds a player page that animates them in Desmos. No copy-pasting. Edge-detection and filter settings above are reused.

▶ Open Desmos Player Download Console Script Download frames.json Download Preview (mp4)

The player runs Desmos right in the page. Alternatively, paste the console script once into the browser console on desmos.com/calculator and it plays there (dv.pause(), dv.play(), dv.go(n), dv.fps(n), dv.mode('poly'), dv.record(2)). Pause to see every curve as a polynomial equation. To screen-record with the equations visible, use Record mode in the player and speed the recording up by the factor it shows.

🎞️ Render Video (graph + equations)

Draws every frame in Desmos with the polynomial equations panel, then stitches the frames into an mp4 at the video's real speed, with the original audio. No screen recording needed. Takes roughly 0.5–1 s per frame. Needs Google Chrome, ffmpeg and Playwright on the machine running this app.
